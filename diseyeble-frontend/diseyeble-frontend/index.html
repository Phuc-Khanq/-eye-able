02 / 05 · How it works

How eye tracking works with a normal webcam

No special hardware. diseyeble finds your face, measures where each iris sits inside the eye, and maps that to a point on the screen.

01

Find the face

Each webcam frame is scanned for a face and 478 landmark points, including both irises.

02

Measure the eyes

The iris centre is compared with the eye corners. Head turn and tilt are measured from the nose and face outline.

03

Calibrate once

You follow a short series of dots on the screen. diseyeble learns how your eye positions map to your screen.

04

Smooth and act

A filter removes jitter and tremor. diseyeble moves the real mouse, and a gaze hold, nod or head shake becomes a click at the pointer.

03 / 05 · Controls

Six ways to control. Zero hands.

Use whichever your body allows. Every input can be switched on or off, and each one has its own sensitivity so tremors and slow movements don't cause false clicks.

Look

Moves the pointer

The pointer follows where you look. Hold your gaze on a button to click it, if you want.

iris + head pose · 30 fps

Hold

Left click

Keep looking at a button. A ring fills around the pointer, and when it is full the button is clicked. Normal blinks never click.

gaze held ~0.9 s · adjustable

Nod

Left click · confirm

A small nod clicks, or says "yes" when diseyeble asks you to confirm a voice command.

pitch ≥ 12° down-up · 600 ms

Shake

Right click · cancel

Shake your head to open the right-click menu, or to say "no" and cancel an action.

yaw ≥ 15° left-right-left

Tilt

Left or right click

Tilt your head towards your left shoulder to left-click, towards your right shoulder to right-click.

roll ≥ 15° and back · 700 ms

Speak

Commands & typing

"Open YouTube", "search for music", "write an email to Mai". Tuned for unclear speech. Always listening, no button to hold.

faster-whisper · dysarthric fine-tune
04 / 05 · Voice

Understands speech that others find hard to follow

Standard voice assistants fail people with slurred speech, a weak voice, or an accent shaped by age or illness. diseyeble listens differently.

  • Trained on unclear speech. The speech model (faster-whisper large-v3-turbo) is fine-tuned on dysarthric speech, so slurred or quiet words are still understood.
  • Always listening. Just speak. There is no button to hold down.
  • Matches meaning, not perfect words. Unclear words are matched to the closest known command.
  • Confirms before acting. When it isn't sure, it shows what it understood and asks you to confirm first.
  • Dictation preview. When a text box is selected, what you say is shown as a preview, then typed in.
  • English today. Vietnamese is planned next.
