# Khang's voice pack script (Oct 24 night). About 160 items, 35 to 45 minutes. Read them in order; you can stop
# with q at any time and carry on later (finished lines are skipped). Most useful parts come first.
# Lines starting with ## tell you HOW to read the lines under them. Read exactly the words shown (punctuation is
# only a guide for how it sounds): the text you see is what the model learns the audio means.
# Tips: sit where you normally sit, the laptop mic at the usual distance, a quiet room. Press Enter, wait a
# beat, read, wait a beat, press Enter. If you stumble or cough, press r to redo.
# Each line must be different from every other line (a finished line is skipped), so the second pass of a
# command differs in punctuation.

## 3. Dictation passages | read like you are dictating a message: natural speed, natural rhythm, keep going through the whole passage, pause only at the full stops
Hi Professor Lee, thank you for meeting with our team yesterday. We have finished the first version of our project, and we would like to show it to you on Monday. Please let me know which time works best for you.
Hey team, quick update from me. The head tracking is working much better after the latest changes, but we still need to test it on a bigger screen. Can someone bring an extra monitor tomorrow morning, and please remember to charge your laptops tonight?
Dear neighbors, we are planning a small garden party on Saturday afternoon at around four o'clock. Everyone is welcome, and we will provide drinks and snacks. If you can bring a dessert to share, that would be wonderful. Please reply by Thursday so we know how many chairs to set up.
Today I need to finish three things. First, pay the electricity bill before noon. Second, call the pharmacy about my prescription. Third, write back to my cousin about the family dinner next weekend.
The meeting started at ten fifteen and lasted about forty minutes. We agreed to move the deadline to Friday, assign the report to Maria, and review the budget again next week. The next meeting is on Wednesday at two.
I just got home and I am exhausted, but the trip was amazing. We hiked up to the lake early in the morning, and the water was so clear that we could see the fish. I will send you the pictures tomorrow, and you have to tell me about your week.
This app lets me use my computer without my hands. I move the cursor with my head, click by blinking for a moment, and type by speaking. It is not perfect yet, but it already saves me a lot of effort every day.
For this assignment, I will explain how neural networks learn from examples. I will start with a simple story about teaching a child to recognize animals, and then I will describe how the computer adjusts its numbers after every mistake.
To make the soup, chop two onions, three carrots, and a few cloves of garlic. Cook them slowly in olive oil for about ten minutes, then add the broth and simmer until everything is soft. Season with salt, pepper, and a little lemon juice before serving.
Our flight leaves Gainesville at six forty five on Thursday morning, so we should arrive at the airport by five. I booked a small hotel near the station for two nights. On Saturday we will take the train to the coast and have lunch by the water.
Please remember to take a short break every hour, drink some water, and stretch your neck and shoulders. If your eyes feel tired, look at something far away for twenty seconds.
I am sorry for the late reply. The past few days have been very busy, and I did not see your message until this morning. I have attached the document you asked for, and I am happy to answer any questions you have.
When I was a child, my grandmother used to tell me stories on the porch every evening. Her voice was slow and warm, and I always fell asleep before the end. Now I try to remember each story and write it down so my children can read them one day.
The system listens to the microphone and watches the camera at the same time. When the mouth is moving, the words are kept, and when it is not, they are ignored. After a short pause, the whole passage is cleaned up and shown on the card before it is typed.
